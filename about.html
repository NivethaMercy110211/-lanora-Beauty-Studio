About Élanora Studio

12 Years of Excellence
Our Story

Born from a Passion for Beauty

Élanora Beauty Studio was founded in 2014 by Priya Sharma with a simple belief: every person deserves to feel beautiful, confident, and at ease. What started as a modest two-chair studio in Chennai has grown into a premier destination for luxury beauty services.

Over 12 years, we've built a team of certified professionals who share a deep love for their craft. Today, Élanora serves over 5,000 clients annually, offering a curated range of hair, skin, and nail services in a welcoming, premium environment.

Your Safety Matters

Hygiene & Safety Standards

At Élanora, hygiene is not an afterthought — it's a foundational commitment. We follow strict sterilization protocols validated by health authorities.

  • All tools sterilized in medical-grade autoclaves after every use
  • Single-use applicators and gloves for all treatments
  • Sanitized stations between every client visit
  • HEPA air filtration systems throughout the studio
  • Certified by Tamil Nadu Board of Health
  • Allergen patch tests offered before all chemical services
